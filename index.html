What is TOP-Eco?

TOP-Eco® is a Japanese-developed Refrigerant Reacting Pipe designed to help improve the efficiency of qualifying commercial air-conditioning systems through a practical retrofit approach. It is installed in the liquid line between the condenser and expansion valve, where its internal structure stirs the refrigerant to promote liquefaction, potentially improving refrigerant condition and supporting better heat transfer. Actual performance may vary depending on the equipment and operating conditions.
